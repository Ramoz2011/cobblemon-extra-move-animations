# Counter

Fighting, physical, priority -5. Counter returns double the physical damage the user just took. The
animation shows **take the hit, turn the force round, strike back**, with Sword/Shield and
Scarlet/Violet as the reference.

Generated by `docs/tools/counter_gen.py` (don't hand-edit the outputs):

- `bedrock/particles/moves/counter/*.particle.json` (25 particles)
- `bedrock/generic/animations/moves/counter.animation.json`: `actor`, `actor_fail`, `target`, `lunge_1..7`
- `data/cobblemon/action_effects/moves/counter.json`
- 4 sound aliases in `sounds.json`: `move.counter.absorb / power / strike / impact` (Cobblemon's own
  Bullet Punch, Protect and Close Combat sounds, re-pitched)

## Reference (official games)

From "#68 Counter - Generations 1-9" (Magiscars Database, YouTube `kh5dB9vT5lM`):

- **Sword/Shield (Urshifu):** the user's whole body flushes red-orange, white steam puffs rise off it,
  then it strikes. The hit is a white-hot burst with orange sparks and radial lines, and the target
  flashes orange.
- **Scarlet/Violet (Quaquaval):** a red-pink body glow (~0.5 s) with white puffs overhead, a punch in
  place, then an orange-white explosion with streaks and sparks and a little lingering orange smoke.
- Neither replays the incoming hit.

## How it fits the battle

Counter has priority -5, so the foe's physical hit has **already played**, with its own animation,
when Counter's animation starts. Nothing replays it. Counter's own animation opens with a short
"absorb" beat that reads as the force it just took landing and being gathered in.

The animation never decides anything itself. It only reads what the battle reported for this move:

| Battle result | Query | What plays |
|---|---|---|
| Hit | `q.hurt` (the move caused a DamageInstruction) | Full sequence, the force crosses to the target, impact, target hit reaction + orange flash |
| Miss | `!q.hurt && q.missed` | Full sequence, the force veers past the target and fades; no impact |
| Failed (not hit physically this turn, so Showdown's `-fail`), or immune (a Ghost target) | neither | `actor_fail`: the guard goes up, holds, and drops. No glow, no strike, and the battle moves on sooner |

These queries exist in both 1.7.3 and 1.8.x. The damage amount is never read, so the effect looks
the same for any Counter. Known limit: a Counter that breaks a **Substitute** produces no
DamageInstruction, so it shows the fail version.

## Timeline (seconds; everything starts in one server tick and runs on the client clock)

| t | Stage |
|---|---|
| 0.00-0.20 | **1. Brace:** crouch, guard up (arms to the chest), a faint warm shimmer rising off the body |
| 0.27 | **2. The force lands:** a small jolt back, a compact white-yellow flash and impact star on the user's front, a few streaks and sparks |
| 0.29-0.45 | ... gathered in: a tight ring closes onto the body, and white-yellow motes are drawn inward |
| 0.40-1.03 | **3. Counterforce:** the model glows red-orange (Java tint, see below), with an orange-white pulse ring, a soft halo, a shiver and white steam puffs off the head |
| 0.56-0.66 | Wind-up: rears back, the punching arm draws back, fast streaks sweep forward past the body |
| 0.70 | **4. Strike:** lunge and punch (lead arm thrust forward); a flash at its front, dust kicked from the feet; the force streaks from its front to the target |
| 0.78 | **Impact:** white core, orange-yellow glow, impact star, radial lines, a small fast shockwave ring, streaks (white, then yellow, then pale red), sparks and a puff of orange smoke; the target is knocked back once, flashes orange, and the HP bar drops |
| 0.78-1.30 | **5. Recovery:** back to the battle stance; everything has faded by ~1.3 s |

## Adapting to any Pokemon

- **Body motion** is on `root_part` (every model has it): crouch, jolt, shiver, rear back and lunge,
  so species without arms still strike with the whole body.
- **Arms** are additive deltas on `arm_left/right`, `left/right_upper_arm` and `left/right_arm`;
  bones a model lacks are skipped (guard on both, then the right arm punches).
- **Lunge distance** is measured on the server (`v.cn_units`, the real gap minus both half-widths,
  in the user's model units) and picks `lunge_1..7` (at most ~0.45 blocks at scale 1). A Pokemon
  standing right next to its target doesn't drive into it.
- **Effect sizes** scale with the entity's width and height (clamped), and every effect sits on the
  body surface or its front, never at the centre, where the model would hide it.
- Species `physical` animations were not used: only 80 models have one and they run ~3 s.

## The Java model tint

`q.user.model_tint(r, g, b, strength, flash, delay, rise, hold, fall)` (any Pokemon entity struct,
so `q.target.model_tint(...)` too). The server sends `ModelTintPayload` to nearby clients, and the
client draws the Pokemon's own model again in its current pose, 1.5% larger and translucent, as a
coloured shell (`client/render/ModelOverlay`, shared with Beat Up's hit flash).

- `flash < 0` mixes in Minecraft's red hurt overlay, so the user reads red-orange whatever its
  colours are (blue Wobbuffet and dark Snorlax were tested). `flash > 0` washes the model towards
  white (used for the target's orange hit flash).
- Missing hook: the call returns 0 and everything else plays. A render error switches tints off for
  the session (logged once).
- Tested on Cobblemon 1.7.3 and 1.8.2.

## Tested in-game (2026-10-22/23)

Cobblemon 1.7.3: Machamp, Wobbuffet, Hitmonchan and Snorlax against a Lv50 Magikarp (Tackle/Flail),
on side and 3/4 cameras. Fail case: "But it failed!" against Splash (twice). Miss case: a
`COUNTER_FORCE_MISS=1` debug build of the generator (never ship it). Beat Up regression check after
its hit flash moved to `ModelOverlay`: afterimages, flash and the "Hit 3 times!" faint all still
work. Cobblemon 1.8.2 runtime check (compileOnly 1.7.3 / runtimeOnly 1.8.2): Hitmonchan hit, tint
and all stages OK, no hook errors.

## Tuning

Edit the constants at the top of `counter_gen.py` and re-run it: the timing (`BRACE` ... `IMPACT`),
the tint (`USER_TINT*`, `TINT0..2`, `TARGET_TINT*`), the lunge (`LUNGE_MAX`) and the palette.
`python3 docs/tools/validate_move.py counter` checks the output.
