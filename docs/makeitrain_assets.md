# Make It Rain — Asset Notes & Integration Guide

Gholdengo's signature Steel move. The animation ships **two custom textures**
(a spinning gold coin and a rotating golden void) plus official Cobblemon
1.7.3 textures and aliased official sounds. Both custom textures are
generated by the scripts in `docs/tools/`, so they can be regenerated or
restyled without an image editor.

## Design

Three phases, matching the mainline Gen 9 move beat-for-beat:

1. **Startup (0 – 1.25 s, actor).** The user dips into an anticipation
   crouch, then leaps with a full 360° celebratory spin. Gold sparkles rush
   inward, a swirling metallic aura spirals into a point above its head,
   spinning coins spiral in to join it, and a volumetric ball of gold swells
   there. The user lands, coils, and **throws the whole hoard straight up**
   — a volumetric gold core plus a spinning coin volley rocket skyward with
   a live trail behind them.
2. **Impact (1.50 – 2.75 s, target).** A golden void tears open ~5.5 blocks
   above the target with a shockwave ring and a halo. A dense torrent of
   spinning coins pours straight down out of it, threaded with gold speed
   streaks. Each wave lands with big comic-style starburst flashes, gold
   shock rings, steel splinters and the official `impact.steel` sound; the
   target plays its own juddering flinch animation throughout.
3. **Follow-through (2.54 s onward, target).** Coins spill across the ground
   and physically bounce to rest, twinkling, before shrinking away as
   drifting golden motes rise off the pile.

## 1. Custom textures (generated, not hand-drawn)

| File | Size | Frames | Generator |
|---|---|---|---|
| `textures/particle/moves/makeitrain_coin.png` | 16×128 | 8 × 16×16 | `docs/tools/makeitrain_coin_texture.py` |
| `textures/particle/moves/makeitrain_void.png` | 32×256 | 8 × 32×32 | `docs/tools/makeitrain_void_texture.py` |

**The coin** is a full revolution of a gold coin about its vertical axis —
face, ellipse, ellipse, near-edge, edge-on, and back again, with the back
half mirrored so the specular highlight crosses over as the reverse face
comes around. Played as a looping flipbook this reads as a genuinely
tumbling 3D coin rather than a flat disc decal, which is what a plain
billboard would give. Per-particle `particle_initial_spin.rotation_rate`
adds in-plane tumble on top; because the flipbook is driven by *particle*
age and coins spawn continuously, they desync naturally with no per-particle
frame offset needed.

**The void** is a dark pit with a bright rim and three gold spiral arms.
Eight frames advance the arms by exactly one third of a turn, so the
flipbook loops seamlessly at any playback rate. Every pixel snaps to one
fixed 10-rung gold ramp, which keeps it crisp pixel-art instead of a smooth
gradient.

To regenerate either:

```sh
python3 docs/tools/makeitrain_coin_texture.py \
  src/main/resources/assets/cobblemon/textures/particle/moves/makeitrain_coin.png
python3 docs/tools/makeitrain_void_texture.py \
  src/main/resources/assets/cobblemon/textures/particle/moves/makeitrain_void.png
```

Both scripts are pure standard-library Python (zlib + struct), no Pillow.

## 2. Sound prompt (ElevenLabs SFX) — optional upgrade

Five events are registered in `assets/cobblemon/sounds.json`, all currently
**aliased to existing official Cobblemon oggs** so the move is audible
immediately. To swap in custom audio, drop the files at
`assets/cobblemon/sounds/move/makeitrain/` and change only the `name` field.

| Event | Current alias | Replacement prompt |
|---|---|---|
| `move.makeitrain.charge` | `swordsdance_actor` @1.15 | "Bright metallic shimmer rising in pitch, like coins being gathered into a pile, ending on a clean ringing chime. ~1.1 s." |
| `move.makeitrain.actor` | `seismictoss_actor` @1.15 | "A heavy whoosh of a huge pile of gold being hurled upward, metallic jangle in the tail. ~0.5 s." |
| `move.makeitrain.void` | `psychic_actor` @0.7 | "A low warped tearing sound as a portal rips open, deep and resonant with a metallic edge. ~0.7 s." |
| `move.makeitrain.coins` | `bulletpunch_target` @1.35 | "A fast dense patter of metal coins striking a hard surface, like a slot machine payout. ~0.6 s." |
| `move.makeitrain.target` | `bulletpunch_target` @0.8 | "One heavy metallic CLANG of a mass of coins landing hard, with a jangling decay. ~0.8 s." |

The per-hit impacts additionally use the official 8-variant
`cobblemon:impact.steel` event and the stock `cobblemon:impact_steel`
particle, so they already sound and read as Steel-type without any custom
audio.

## 5. Gotchas worth knowing

- **`particles_alpha` is a cutout material in Cobblemon** — 688 of the 689
  official particles that use it never animate alpha. Anything on
  `particles_alpha` (here: every coin, because solid opaque coins need real
  depth sorting in a dense torrent) must fade by **shrinking its size
  curve**, not by ramping tint alpha, or it will pop instead of fade. Use
  `particles_blend` when a genuine alpha fade is needed.
- `emitter_shape_disc.plane_normal` wants the string `"y"`, not `[0,1,0]`.
- Multiple particle effects at one animation timestamp must be a JSON
  **list** (`seismictoss`/`bulldoze` do this); a bare dict silently allows
  only one, and duplicate keys are invalid JSON.
- Texture files live under `textures/particle/` (singular) on disk but are
  referenced as `textures/particles/` (plural) in particle JSON — Cobblemon
  remaps.
